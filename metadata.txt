It implements the complete survey-production toolset: (1) Lot Traverse Plotting with tie-line geometry, closure diagnostics, OCR extraction, and multi-format CSV import. (2) Coordinate Point Ingestion (NEZL, NEL, NEZ, NE, PNEZL, KDMS) with sequential boundary string joining (seqline). (3) Boundary Bearing and Distance Annotation in Meters (m) and Feet (') with automatic upside-down text flipping. (4) Cartographic Survey Grid generation with collar coordinates and tick marks. (5) Delaunay TIN Surface modeling with 3D area and slope analytics. (6) Topographic Contour generation with cubic spline smoothing and hypsometric tinting. (7) Modular QGIS Processing Provider.
